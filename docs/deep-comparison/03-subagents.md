# 03. Subagents

_Deep tit-for-tat: 10 "Claude Code subagents" collections vs. this template's 2 agents. Objective: **specialized subagent definitions**. Snapshot 2026-07-02; star counts omitted (this env's proxy inflates them)._

This template ships **2 agents by deliberate design (ADR-022)**: `scout` (haiku, read-only Read/Grep/Glob investigator that keeps repo-wide exploration off the main context+bill) and `senior-code-reviewer` (sonnet, post-push breadth pass over four defect classes the diff-gate can't see: cross-cutting invariant completeness, output-side trust boundaries, operational drift, architecture — shipped with a **portable installer**, `scripts/install-senior-review.mjs` / `tools/senior-review/`). The field's whole value proposition is the opposite: **breadth** — 6 to 194 specialist personas covering every language, framework, and department. So on the raw objective ("how many specialized subagents can I pick from"), the collections win by construction; where the template competes is on the two axes almost none of them touch — **model-tiering as a cost lever** (only wshobson and VoltAgent do it well) and **a portable cross-repo installer** (essentially unique here). The honest read: adopt specialists from the field where a real gap exists (a Prisma/Next.js/Auth.js reviewer would be on-stack), but keep the minimal spine.
