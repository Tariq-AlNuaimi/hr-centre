## 02. Skills collections

**Objective:** provide reusable SKILL.md capabilities.

Two very different kinds of repo sit in this category, and they must be judged differently against the "reusable" objective. Four are **producers** — they ship actual, portable SKILL.md capabilities (obra/superpowers, anthropics/skills, mattpocock/skills, addyosmani/agent-skills) — and two are large hosted libraries (ComposioHQ, alirezarezvani); the other four are **curated indexes** (VoltAgent, travisvn, BehiSecc, karanb192) that link out rather than ship anything. On the literal objective — *reusable, portable* SKILL.md capabilities — the producers genuinely beat us: our 25 skills are excellent but deliberately welded to one stack (Next.js/Prisma/Auth.js/AntD + Vercel/Neon), so they are not "reusable" in the sense this category means. Where we win is the opposite axis: our skills *provision infrastructure* and *enforce gates* (`/ship`, `/deploy`, `/scaffold-entity`), which none of these portable collections do. The honest reading is that this is the one category where the field is designed for the exact thing we chose not to optimize for — and CLAUDE.md already concedes it by deferring to Superpowers.
