## 15. Benchmarking

**Objective:** benchmarking in all three senses — software/perf (HTTP + CLI throughput/latency), AI-agent/LLM (SWE-bench-style resolve rates on real GitHub issues), and market/competitive (survey existing products before building). The distinction is the whole story here: the ten repos overwhelmingly do perf or LLM benchmarking, both of which measure a *finished system's* numbers. This template does neither — its only benchmarking is **market/competitive**: CLAUDE.md's "Market benchmark before building" standing instruction (web-search existing products/libraries/starters, summarize trade-offs, add a "market scan" section to every spec) and `/uat` Full mode's "peer + enterprise benchmark" phase. That is a legitimate but orthogonal sense of "benchmark," so for most rows the honest verdict is "they win their sense, we win ours" rather than a head-to-head. A prototype rail that ships disposable internal tools has no standing need for load-test rigs or model-eval harnesses; it needs to not reinvent a product that already exists, which is exactly what it gates on.
