# 17. Functional testing

**Objective:** functional / E2E / UAT / browser-automation / BDD.

**Synthesis.** This is the template's strongest and most Claude-native category: `/uat` (lean smoke + a 7-phase full deep audit with a permission matrix, per-entity CRUD journeys, a peer benchmark, and an HTML+PDF report), `/manual-uat` (the agent drives a live browser as each role — happy + worst path, RBAC reconciled against live grants, a completion-contract coverage matrix), and `/design-qa` (per-state screenshots + axe + a scored design diff) already drive **`microsoft/playwright-mcp` (#4)** and sit inside the gated `/qa` rubric — so most of the field is either our _foundation_ (Playwright, playwright-mcp) or a thinner subset of what we ship (the two `playwright-skill` repos). The genuine gaps are structural, not capability: our exploratory `/manual-uat` walk is thrown away as a report instead of captured as a **reusable regression spec** (executeautomation's codegen-session), our blueprint scenarios are prose rather than **executable Given/When/Then** (cucumber), and the live browser context we feed the agent is un-trimmed against the token-thrift policy (willmarple's TrimmedResponse). The honest read: we win the objective for _auditing a known app_, and the fair borrows are all about durability and token-efficiency, not raw automation.
