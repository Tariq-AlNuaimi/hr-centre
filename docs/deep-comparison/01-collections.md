## 01. Curated / Awesome collections

**Synthesis.** These ten repos exist to do one thing this template deliberately does *not*: aggregate and help you *discover* the whole Claude Code ecosystem — hundreds of skills/agents/hooks/commands/MCP servers, browsable, searchable, one-click installable from a marketplace or CSV index. For that objective the honest verdict is that essentially every repo here beats us, because we are not a public collection at all — we're a single, opinionated, pre-curated working set (25 lifecycle skills, 2 agents, 5 hooks, 10 ESLint rules) wired to one stack. Our only "discovery" surface is *internal* (the self-documenting SKILL.md list, `docs/REPO-MAP.md`, `docs/agent-memory.md`), which answers "where is X in *this* repo," not "what exists in the ecosystem." The right posture is to *consume* these directories to source new capabilities and to get *listed in* the canonical one (#1), not to compete on breadth.
